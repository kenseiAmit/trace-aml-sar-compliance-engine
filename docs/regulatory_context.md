# TRACE Regulatory Compliance & SAR Drafting Guide

## Statutory & Regulatory Framework

TRACE complies with United States Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF) regulations administered by the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) and the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC).

### Applicable Authorities & Citations

1. **General SAR Filing Obligation**:
   - `31 CFR 1020.320`: Mandatory reporting by banks of suspicious transactions involving aggregate funds of $5,000 or more (or $25,000 or more if the suspect cannot be identified).

2. **Structuring to Evade Reporting**:
   - `31 U.S.C. 5324`: Structuring transactions to evade Currency Transaction Reporting (CTR) requirements under 31 CFR 1010.311 ($10,000 cash threshold).
   - Applied when a subject conducts multiple cash deposits or withdrawals just below $10,000 (e.g., $9,400 to $9,800) across consecutive days or multiple branch locations.

3. **Money Laundering**:
   - `18 U.S.C. 1956`: Laundering of monetary instruments involving funds derived from specified unlawful activities.
   - Applied when transactional velocity exhibits layering patterns (e.g., large incoming wires rapidly disbursed via multiple outgoing transfers without clear commercial rationale).

4. **Sanctions & Prohibited Entities**:
   - `31 CFR Part 501` (and `31 CFR Part 500 et seq.`): OFAC reporting and asset-blocking requirements.
   - Applied when counterparties match designated sanctions lists (SDN list) or high-risk jurisdictions.

5. **Fraud**:
   - `18 U.S.C. 1344` (Bank Fraud) and `18 U.S.C. 1343` (Wire Fraud).
   - Applied in account takeover, elder financial exploitation, or deceptive transactional schemes.

## SAR Narrative Drafting Standards (FinCEN Form 111)

### The Five Essential Elements (5 W's)
Every SAR narrative generated by TRACE must document:
1. **Who**: Full legal name, customer ID, account numbers, and verified age/demographics. Verified case records take absolute precedence over analytical claims.
2. **What**: The specific transactional instruments employed (cash deposits, wire transfers, ACH) and **exact monetary amounts** involved.
3. **When**: Precise chronological timeframe, including individual dates or date ranges.
4. **Where**: Branches, channels, or geographic locations involved in the activity.
5. **Why (Suspicion)**: Objective, qualified description of why the pattern warrants suspicion, referencing deviation from established customer baseline activity.

### Language & Tone Guidelines
- **Objective & Qualified Phrasing**: Use legally sound, qualified phrasing:
  - *Compliant*: "The observed velocity is consistent with potential structuring to circumvent reporting obligations."
  - *Non-Compliant*: "The customer structured deposits designed to evade taxes."
- **Prohibited Presumptions**: Do not treat demographic attributes or account recency as standalone proof of criminality. Always ground assessments in transaction volume, velocity, and baseline comparisons.
- **Strict Word Limit**: FinCEN Form 111 summaries generated by the Compliance Officer must remain under **120 words** for concise operational triage.

## Multi-Factor Substantive Validation Rules

The TRACE validation engine enforces substantive completeness before authorizing document export:
1. **Word Count**: Exactly between 10 and 120 words.
2. **Subject Check**: Verified customer identifier or name present in text.
3. **Activity Check**: Core transactional verbs present.
4. **Timeframe Check**: Specific calendar dates or structured time intervals.
5. **Actual Amounts**: Explicit dollar amounts corresponding to actual transaction records; threshold-only values ($10,000 alone) or trivial amounts ($1) are rejected.
6. **Indicators Check**: Clear presence of recognized AML typologies.
7. **Statutory Citations**: Verified citations strictly matching the observed typology.
